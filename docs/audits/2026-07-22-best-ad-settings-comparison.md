## Conclusion

There is no campaign, audience, optimization, conversion, placement, URL, CTA,
pixel or text-stack setting that explains the winner's lead volume. The winner
and the new ads are almost entirely configured the same.

The most defensible inference is that `Roadmap Complete` is winning because of
its video creative and accumulated delivery history, not because it has a
special targeting or bidding advantage.

The one setting worth aligning is automatic translation: the winner has both
Text and Voice translation off, while the inspected new ad has Voice on. The
new ad also permits more automatic creative enhancement. If controlled creative
testing is the priority, match the winner by turning translation off and
reducing enhancements. Do not change the shared campaign or ad-set settings.

The new ads had effectively no exposure in the completed 7-20 July window, so
their lack of leads in this comparison is not evidence of underperformance.
