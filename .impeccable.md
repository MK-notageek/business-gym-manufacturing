## Design Context

### Users
New Zealand manufacturing business owners arriving from a lead-magnet ad. They want a practical diagnosis of factory profit leaks and a clear path to book a free Growth Assessment with a PBA advisor.

### Brand Personality
Direct, practical, energetic. PBA should feel commercially experienced, action-oriented, and grounded in the realities of running a factory.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the established dark PBA Trade Map landing-page system, including its typography, colours, roadmap mock-up, and linear conversion flow. The manufacturer funnel should feel like the same proven family, adapted to factory owners.

### Design Principles

- Put the roadmap mock-up directly under the headline on mobile.
- Keep the form and booking mechanics unchanged while adapting layout.
- Make the Growth Assessment the clear next step after roadmap delivery.
- Preserve accessibility, tracking, calendar prefill, and responsive behaviour.
- Use testimonials and reviews as supporting proof after the decision point.
